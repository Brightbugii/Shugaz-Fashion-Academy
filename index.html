What you need to join

No experience? No problem. Here is what to have ready.

Requirements
  • A phone with WhatsApp and Telegram
  • Your class fee paid in full before you join the group
  • Willingness to practise between classes
  • Students under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent
What to bring or buy
  • Measuring tape, tailor's chalk and fabric scissors
  • Pins, needles, thread and a seam ripper
  • Notebook and pen
  • Fabric and boning for practice pieces (list sent after enrolment)

Dos and don'ts

Simple rules that keep every class focused and fair.

Do
  • Arrive on time with your tools
  • Wear fitted, comfortable clothes so we can check your measurements
  • Practise the lesson before the next class
  • Ask questions in the class group
  • Keep your receipt until you finish the course
Don't
  • Don't send only a greeting in a message. Write your full question so we can answer at once.
  • Don't share class videos or patterns outside the group
  • Don't skip classes without telling us first
  • Don't bring guests unless we have agreed
  • Don't expect refunds after classes begin

Fees are not refundable once classes begin. If you cannot attend, tell us before your class starts and we will move you to the next class.
